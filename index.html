A living body of water

Touch the surface. Let it answer.

Thousands of drifting particles respond to every gesture with gentle ripples. Shape the swell, choose the hour of light, and pause the tide whenever you need stillness.

Drag anywhere on the water

Dusk

Shift from dawn teal to midnight ink.

Gentle

Raise or soften the wave height.

Tide

Hold the surface still.

Made of drifting particles — no two waves alike.
